## Already matches
- Step bullets and "Step N of 3" label (done = check, current = ringed dot, next = gray dot), R1 and SE agree (`sign-up.js:78-88`).
- Step 1: title, subtitle, intro, field order and widths, Country with US flag, "Only two more steps to go!", Next disabled (`disabled:bg-blue-300`, dark `disabled:bg-blue-500/40`) until both consents accepted. Error text "<Field> is required" with (!) icon; "Zip / Postal Code" typo deliberately normalised (flow doc).
- Step 2: heading, intro, info box, radio list, selected card = 2px blue-600 border (matches SE 3.1), "No, this isn't my business." secondary md with info text, Back / Next Step.
- Step 3: field set, read-only Email (gray-50), Phone prefilled, spinner on submit.
- Sign-in: card, code boxes behaviour (paste, arrows, backspace), info tooltip, soft Resend Code, top alert with the shared X (rule 8), locked, Microsoft picker, business list + search, platform cards, full-page loading, Enroll now link.
- Button hover/active/disabled/focus-visible/dark all present on primary, soft, secondary, link, close X.
- Copy icons: only in the Contact Us dialog, wired with `OBModal.copyButton` (no unwired copy icons on these pages).
- sign.html: no SE frames in the Speedy Signup section; its R1 frames (29624:*) were checked by TCDM-5251 and the Nov 24 captures look right. SE Submit Modal-Steps (`5280:338858`) are not dumped in `se-states.md`, so any state check for sign.html (Confirm disabled/loading, Confirm Acceptance disabled) stays open: R1 frame 29624:12407 shows Confirm Acceptance as blue-300 disabled, same as code's `disabled:bg-blue-300`.
